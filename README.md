## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
EXPLANATION:
* A mobile or web applications marketplace where the farmers list produce directly
* Buyers search and order it directly
* Supports local transports and bulk order
* AI used to find what crops are needed
* supports best delivary
  
HOW TO ADDRESS THE PROBLEM:
*Problem:manny middle mans are they so farmeres earn less and consumers pay more
*direct selling removes middleman to reduce loses
*helps the farmers to plan what to sell,so less loss
*route optimization lowers the delivery cost,so price stay low
*Result:better price for farmers ,lower prices for consumers

INNOVATION:
*Direct selling ,AI in one platform instead of only a listing app
*regional language and voice supports fo rural farmers
*fair price guidance
